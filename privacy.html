Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

What we collect

  • Purchase details: name, email and billing details you give at checkout. Card details are handled by Stripe; we never see or store full card numbers.
  • Messages you send us: emails and anything you share while we help you.
  • Setup and Care Plan customers: access to the accounts you authorize us to configure (for example n8n, Twilio, Google Sheets), used only to deliver the service.

Website analytics

We count page visits and clicks on buy buttons to see which pages help visitors. We do not use cookies or tracking pixels for this, and we do not store IP addresses or anything that identifies you. We keep only daily totals, such as how many people viewed a page and which website sent them.

How we use it

To deliver what you bought, provide support, send receipts and service messages, and meet legal and tax obligations. We don't sell your personal information.

Your customers' data

The automations process your customers' names and phone numbers inside your accounts (your Google Sheet, your Twilio account). For Care Plan customers, we access that data only to operate and fix your automations.

Service providers

We use Stripe (payments) and Vercel (website hosting), and, for Care Plan customers, the automation providers you use. Each processes data under its own privacy terms.

Retention and your choices

We keep purchase records as long as needed for tax and accounting. You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information by emailing leadwrenchsupport@gmail.com.

Cookies

This website doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. Stripe may set cookies during checkout for fraud prevention.
