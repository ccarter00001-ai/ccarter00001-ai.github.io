Questions

I'm not technical. Can I still use this?

Yes. Choose Done-For-You and we set everything up and test it with you.

Do I need a new phone number?

You get a local Twilio number. Forward your current line to it, or put it on your truck and ads.

Is texting customers allowed?

Yes for people who contact you or are your customers, with an opt-out in every message. Our guide covers US carrier registration (A2P 10DLC).

What do I need to run it?

An n8n account (self-hosted or n8n Cloud), a Twilio account and a Google account. With Done-For-You or the Care Plan, we handle it.

What's your refund policy?

Setup comes with a 7-day working guarantee, and the DIY kit can be refunded within 14 days if it doesn't work for you. Full details are in our refund policy.
