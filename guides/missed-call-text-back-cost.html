How much does missed call text back cost?

There are two ways to get missed-call text-back: pay a monthly platform, or own a simple automation that runs on your own phone number. Here is what each costs.

Option 1: monthly platforms

Most text-back and review platforms aimed at home-service businesses charge somewhere around $97 to $450 a month, often bundled with reviews, a web chat widget and a shared inbox. Over a year, that is roughly $1,200 to $5,400, and it never stops.

Option 2: your own automation

  • Phone number: a local Twilio number is a little over $1 a month.
  • Texts: under a cent per message segment, plus small carrier fees. A busy shop sending a few hundred texts a month usually pays a few dollars.
  • Business texting registration (A2P 10DLC): one-time brand and campaign fees plus a small monthly campaign fee. Check Twilio's current numbers.
  • Automation tool: n8n self-hosted is free; n8n Cloud has a monthly plan.
  • Setup: your time, a one-time kit, or a one-time done-for-you install.

Which is cheaper?

For most small contractors, owning it is far cheaper after the first couple of months. A platform makes sense if you want an all-in-one inbox and someone else handling everything for a monthly fee.

What is one missed call worth?

If you miss 8 calls a week, half are new customers, you would win 40% of them, and your average job is $450, that is about $28,000 a year. Run your own numbers in the free calculator.

Questions

Is there a free missed call text back?

Some phone carriers and apps offer basic auto-replies, but they usually cannot alert you, log leads or follow up. Building your own with Twilio costs only a few dollars a month to run.

Are there hidden fees with Twilio?

Expect the number fee, per-message fees, carrier pass-through fees and business texting registration fees. Twilio lists them on its pricing pages.
