How contractors get more Google reviews

Reviews decide who gets the call on Google Maps. Most happy customers will leave one, but only if you ask at the right moment and make it one tap.

1. Ask the same day

The best time is a few hours after the job, while the fixed AC or dry basement is fresh. Asking a week later gets a fraction of the reviews.

2. Send a direct link

In your Google Business Profile, find "Ask for reviews" and copy the short link. It opens the review box directly.

3. Keep the message short

Hi [Name], thanks for choosing [Business] today! If we earned it, a quick Google review helps a small local business a lot: [link]

4. Ask everyone, once

Do not filter who gets asked based on whether you think they are happy. Google's policies prohibit selectively asking for positive reviews. Ask every customer once and never repeat.

5. Reply to every review

Thank positive reviewers by name and the job. Answer negative ones calmly and offer to fix it offline. Future customers read your replies.

6. Automate it

The LeadWrench review request automation texts your review link each evening to customers whose job you marked Completed, once per customer. It is one of the five automations in the $79 kit.

Questions

Can I offer a discount for reviews?

No. Google prohibits incentivized reviews. Just ask at the right time with a direct link.

How many reviews do I need?

There is no magic number, but steady new reviews matter. Asking every customer usually adds several a month for a busy shop.
