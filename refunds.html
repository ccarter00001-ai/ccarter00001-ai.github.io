Refund Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

DIY Kit

If the kit doesn't work for you, email us within 14 days of purchase and tell us what went wrong. We'll try to help first. If we can't fix it, we refund you in full.

Done-For-You Setup

We aim to have your automations live within 7 days of receiving the account access we need. If the missed-call text-back isn't working within 7 days of us receiving that access, you get a full refund. Delays caused by third-party approvals outside our control, such as US carrier texting registration (A2P 10DLC), pause the 7-day clock. We'll tell you if that happens.

Care Plan

Cancel anytime. Cancellation stops future charges, and you keep service until the end of the paid month. We don't refund partial months, except where required by law.

Third-party costs

Fees you pay directly to other providers (for example Twilio texting fees, carrier registration fees, n8n or Google subscriptions) are between you and those providers and aren't refundable by LeadWrench.

How to request a refund

Email leadwrenchsupport@gmail.com with your order email. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method, usually within 5–10 business days.
